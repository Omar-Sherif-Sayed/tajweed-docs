Dear King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex,

I am building a free application called "Tajweed - The Noble Quran and Its Sciences" for the web, Android and iPhone. It is entirely free, has no advertisements, no sign-in and no accounts, collects no data about its users, and has no commercial purpose. Its code is open source on GitHub. It shows the Quran in the narration of Hafs from Asim, laid out like the printed Madinah mushaf, with recitations, tafsir, adhkar and more, with an interface in 22 languages, for Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah.

In everything we take from a source we commit to: copying the text exactly as it is, with no change or shortening; showing the source, its author and its link to the user; and applying at once any correction you send us.

We wish to show the Quran in the application with the page fonts of the Madinah mushaf (the 1405H print, in the hand of Sheikh Uthman Taha) issued by the Complex, so that every page matches the printed mushaf exactly. We found no download link or terms of use for these fonts on the Complex's website (the fonts page says "coming soon").

We kindly ask for permission to use these fonts inside the application (embedded in it, unmodified, never sold or distributed separately), and for your answer on:
1. Whether this is possible, and under which conditions.
2. The credit line you would like shown in the application.
3. Whether the Complex provides an official copy of the fonts and their files that we should use instead of the copies in circulation.
