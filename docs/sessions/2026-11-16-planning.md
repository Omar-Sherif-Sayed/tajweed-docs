### Findings reported to the author

- PrimeNG 22 is no longer open source (free "Community" licence with a yearly key).
- In Angular, "LTS" is the security-only phase of the previous major; the version to use is the active one, 22.
- Android development is Kotlin-first; an iPhone application needs a Mac to build and upload.
- A closed iPhone application can only play a notification sound shorter than 30 seconds — not the full adhan.
- محمد رفعت: 31 surahs only · البهتيمي: no source · عبد الباسط: no muallim mushaf · ماهر المعيقلي: muallim 38 surahs ·
  الشيخ نورين recites the riwayah الدوري عن أبي عمرو, not Hafs.
- The YouTube playlist (المصحف المعلم للأطفال — الحصري) cannot be extracted, and its player can show advertisements.
- Printed mushafs do not agree on which words are coloured besides «الله»; a plain text search would also colour
  words such as «اللهو».
- The rights to recordings, tafsirs, translations and book editions are the main risk.
